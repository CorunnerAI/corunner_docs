Welcome to the Corunner documentation. Corunner is an AI-powered business operating system that lives inside Slack and Microsoft Teams. It reads context from the tools your team already uses, routes work to a team of specialist agents, and takes action asynchronously with human approval on the things that matter.

This documentation covers the customer-facing product: how to connect Corunner to your workspace, chat with the agents, wire up integrations, and administer memory, standups, and governance from the web app.

## What Corunner is

Corunner sits above your tools and coordinates how work moves between them. Instead of another tab to open, it runs where your team already talks:

- **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams** for every conversation with `@Corunner`.
- The **web app** for onboarding, connecting integrations, managing agents and standups, browsing memory, and setting governance policies.
- Any action that touches a production system, a customer, or a person asks for **human approval by default**, right inside chat.

## What is in the web app

The web app exists for the things you do rarely. Your team does not need to learn it, your admins do, once.

## Who this documentation is for

- **Everyone on the team**: how to talk to Corunner in Slack or Teams.
- **Workspace admins**: how to install Corunner, connect integrations, and configure agents.
- **Organization owners and admins**: how to set governance policies and manage seats.

<Note>
  Corunner requires a workspace admin to install the app in Slack or Microsoft Teams. Members can sign in once the workspace is connected.
</Note>
