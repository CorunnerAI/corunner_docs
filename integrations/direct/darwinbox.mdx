Darwinbox is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner sync HR, attendance, and org data. Once connected, agents get organizational context and attendance awareness so they route work correctly.

## What Corunner does with Darwinbox

Agents read employee records and attendance to build org context and to avoid pinging people who are marked absent. Standups can use attendance to skip prompts for people who are out.

## What you get

- Read employees
- Read attendance

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Darwinbox. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Org context
- Attendance awareness

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Darwinbox** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
