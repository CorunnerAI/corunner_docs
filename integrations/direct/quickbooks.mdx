QuickBooks is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner pull invoices, expenses, and accounting data. Once connected, the Finance Agent uses QuickBooks data for finance signals and invoice context.

## What Corunner does with QuickBooks

Agents read invoices, expenses, and accounts to answer questions like "which invoices are outstanding?" or "what did we spend on X last month?" without opening QuickBooks.

## What you get

- Read invoices
- Read expenses
- Read accounts

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A QuickBooks Online admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Finance signals
- Invoice context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **QuickBooks** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
