Chargebee is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read subscriptions and invoices. Once connected, agents send billing context and MRR signals into chat.

## What Corunner does with Chargebee

Agents read subscription and invoice data to answer billing questions and to track MRR movement over time.

## What you get

- Read subscriptions
- Read invoices

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Chargebee. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Billing context
- MRR signals

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Chargebee** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
