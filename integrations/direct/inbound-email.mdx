Inbound Email is a Direct Connection that captures explicitly recorded decisions from forwarded email. It is a one-way inbound channel: emails you forward to a Corunner address are received, verified, and staged as decision-capture events for human review before they enter memory.

<Note>
  This is not a general email inbox integration. It does not read your Gmail or Outlook inbox. It only receives signed decision emails you forward to Corunner.
</Note>

## What Corunner does with Inbound Email

Corunner listens for signed email events and stages them as **decision approvals**. A curator reviews each staged decision and either captures it into memory or rejects it. Use this when a decision was made over email and you want it recorded without retyping it in chat.

## What you get

- Receive signed email events
- Stage decision approvals

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Corunner. Configure the forwarding rules in your email provider to send the target messages to the Corunner inbound address.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Email decision capture

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Inbound Email** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
