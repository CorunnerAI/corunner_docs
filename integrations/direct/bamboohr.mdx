BambooHR is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner pull org structure, time off, and people data. Once connected, agents know who is on which team, who is out this week, and how to reason about capacity.

## What Corunner does with BambooHR

Agents use employee and org-chart data to route work correctly and to build organizational memory. Time-off data feeds availability signals so agents avoid pinging people who are out of office.

## What you get

- Read employees
- Read time off
- Read org chart

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in BambooHR. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Time-off awareness
- Capacity planning

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **BambooHR** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
