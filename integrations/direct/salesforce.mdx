Salesforce is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read your CRM: opportunities, accounts, and activities. Once connected, agents surface pipeline signals, deal health, and account context inside chat, so revenue teams can ask about a deal without switching tools.

## What Corunner does with Salesforce

Agents read pipeline data to answer questions like "what's at risk this quarter?" and to flag deals that are slipping. Account context feeds customer-history lookups in support and success conversations.

## What you get

- Read opportunities
- Read accounts
- Read activities

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Salesforce admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Pipeline signals
- Deal health

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Salesforce** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
