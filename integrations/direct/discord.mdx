Discord is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner post notifications into your Discord server and read channel signals for community awareness. Discord is not one of Corunner's primary chat providers — for that, use [Slack](/chat/slack) or [Microsoft Teams](/chat/microsoft-teams).

## What Corunner does with Discord

Corunner uses Discord to send notifications and pick up community signals, for example when a channel is discussing an outage or a launch. Discord is one-way in most flows: agents post updates in the channels you specify.

## What you get

- Post messages
- Read channels

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Discord admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Notifications
- Community signals

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Discord** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
