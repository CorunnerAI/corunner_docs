Zoho CRM is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read leads, deals, and accounts from your Zoho CRM organization. Once connected, agents surface pipeline signals and lead context in chat.

## What Corunner does with Zoho CRM

Agents read deals and leads to answer questions about pipeline shape, deal movement, and lead attention. Account data provides context when a customer name comes up in chat.

## What you get

- Read deals
- Read leads
- Read accounts

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Zoho CRM admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Pipeline signals
- Lead context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Zoho CRM** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
