NetSuite is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read financials, orders, and other ERP records. Once connected, the Finance Agent uses NetSuite for finance signals and revenue context.

## What Corunner does with NetSuite

Agents pull transactions and records so they can answer questions about revenue trends, orders in flight, and account status without opening NetSuite. Data feeds the Finance Agent and downstream reporting workflows.

## What you get

- Read transactions
- Read records

## Authentication

Connect with a **service account**. Your NetSuite admin creates a service account (or integration record) with the required read scopes and supplies its credentials in the connection card.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Finance signals
- Revenue context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **NetSuite** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
