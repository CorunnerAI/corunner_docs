Quantive StrategyAI is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read OKRs and strategic goals from Quantive. Once connected, agents score goal confidence, correlate risks to OKRs, and drive executive intelligence workflows.

## What Corunner does with Quantive StrategyAI

Agents read OKRs, strategic goals, and confidence scores. This feeds goal-confidence scoring, correlates operational risks to OKR outcomes, and produces the kind of digests leadership uses to steer the quarter.

## What you get

- Read OKRs
- Read strategic goals
- Read confidence scores

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Quantive. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Goal confidence scoring
- OKR risk correlation
- Executive digests

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Quantive StrategyAI** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
