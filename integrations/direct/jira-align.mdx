Jira Align is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read portfolios, programs, and epics from Jira Align. Once connected, agents provide portfolio alignment, program risk, and strategy-to-delivery traceability.

## What Corunner does with Jira Align

Agents read portfolios, programs, and epics to answer strategic questions about how in-flight work maps to portfolios, and to flag program risk when execution slips.

## What you get

- Read portfolios
- Read programs
- Read epics

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Jira Align. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Portfolio alignment
- Program risk
- Strategy-to-delivery traceability

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Jira Align** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
