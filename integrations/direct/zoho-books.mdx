Zoho Books is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read invoices, expenses, and contacts, and write invoices and expenses back into Zoho Books. This is one of the few finance integrations where Corunner can also create records with approval.

## What Corunner does with Zoho Books

Agents read your ledger for finance signals, invoice context, and AR/AP visibility. When granted approval, the Finance Agent can create invoices and expenses on your behalf so admin-heavy work does not accumulate.

## What you get

- Read invoices
- Read expenses
- Read contacts
- Create invoices
- Create expenses

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Zoho Books admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Finance signals
- Invoice context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Zoho Books** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
