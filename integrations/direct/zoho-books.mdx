Zoho Books is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read invoices, expenses, and contacts, and write invoices and expenses back into Zoho Books. This is one of the few finance integrations where Corunner can also create records with approval.

## What Corunner does with Zoho Books

Agents read your ledger for finance signals, invoice context, and AR/AP visibility. When granted approval, the Finance Agent can create invoices and expenses on your behalf so admin-heavy work does not accumulate.

## What you get

- Read invoices
- Read expenses
- Read contacts
- Create invoices
- Create expenses

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Zoho Books admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Finance signals
- Invoice context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Zoho Books** under the **Direct Connections** tab.

## Connect Zoho Books

When you click **Connect Zoho Books**, Corunner opens the connection dialog and redirects you to Zoho. If the redirect does not start automatically, click **Continue to Zoho Books**.

<Frame caption="Continue from Corunner to Zoho Books to approve access.">
  ![Connect Zoho Books dialog in Corunner with a Continue to Zoho Books button and credential security notice](/images/integrations/direct/zoho/zoho.png)
</Frame>

Corunner never receives your Zoho password. It receives only the scopes you approve, and you can revoke access at any time by disconnecting the integration.

## Sign in to Zoho

Zoho Accounts asks you to sign in before you can review and approve the requested access.

<Frame caption="Sign in to your Zoho account to continue connecting Zoho Books.">
  ![Zoho Accounts sign-in page with an email or mobile number field and alternative sign-in providers](/images/integrations/direct/zoho/zoho1.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your Zoho account details">
    Enter the email address or mobile number associated with the Zoho Books organization you want to connect, then click **Next**. You can also use one of the available identity providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the requested access">
    Confirm that you are connecting the intended Zoho organization. Review the scopes Corunner requests for invoices, expenses, and contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Approve access in Zoho. Zoho redirects you to Corunner, where the Zoho Books card shows the connected state.
  </Step>
</Steps>
