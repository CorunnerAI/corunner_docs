HubSpot is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner sync deals, contacts, and pipelines from your HubSpot CRM. Once connected, agents surface deal health and pipeline signals so revenue teams can ask about deals in chat without opening HubSpot.

## What Corunner does with HubSpot

Agents read your deals, contacts, and pipelines. They correlate deal movement with meetings, tickets, and Slack chatter so you can spot deals that are slipping or contacts who need attention.

## What you get

- Read deals
- Read contacts
- Read pipelines

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A HubSpot admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Deal health
- Pipeline signals

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **HubSpot** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
