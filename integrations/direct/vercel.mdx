Vercel is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner track deployments and preview environments across your Vercel projects. Once connected, agents surface deploy tracking and preview signals in chat.

## What Corunner does with Vercel

Agents read your project list and deployment history, so they can tell the team when a preview is ready, when a production deploy shipped, and when a deploy failed. Deploy history also feeds incident context.

## What you get

- Read deployments
- Read projects

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Vercel team member with the right permissions approves access during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Deploy tracking
- Preview signals

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Vercel** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
