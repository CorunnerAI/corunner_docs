Zoom is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read meetings and recordings. Once connected, agents can generate recording summaries and pull meeting context into chat.

## What Corunner does with Zoom

Agents read meetings and recordings to build meeting context, produce concise summaries, and pull action items out of recorded calls into your project tools.

## What you get

- Read meetings
- Read recordings

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Zoom account admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Meeting context
- Recording summaries

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Zoom** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
