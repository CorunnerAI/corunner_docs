Harness is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner monitor CI/CD pipelines and deployments. Once connected, agents can surface deploy status and pipeline health in chat, so teams do not have to jump into the Harness UI to see what shipped.

## What Corunner does with Harness

Agents read your pipelines and deployments to keep the team aware of what is in flight and what shipped, and to correlate deploys with incidents when things go wrong.

## What you get

- Read pipelines
- Read deployments

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Harness. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Deploy tracking
- Pipeline health

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Harness** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
