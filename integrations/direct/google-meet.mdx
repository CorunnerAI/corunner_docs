Google Meet is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read meetings and transcripts. Once connected, agents pull call summaries and meeting context into chat.

## What Corunner does with Google Meet

Agents read meetings and transcripts to summarise calls, extract action items, and feed meeting context into standups and other conversations.

## What you get

- Read meetings
- Read transcripts

## Authentication

Connect with OAuth. A Google Workspace admin approves the scopes during the connect flow.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Meeting context
- Call summaries

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Google Meet** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
