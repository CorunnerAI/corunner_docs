Perdoo is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read OKRs, initiatives, and progress from your Perdoo workspace. Once connected, agents correlate blockers to OKRs and score delivery confidence against your goals.

## What Corunner does with Perdoo

Agents read OKRs, initiatives, and progress to align operational work with strategic outcomes. When a blocker or delivery risk appears, agents can trace it back to the OKR it threatens.

## What you get

- Read OKRs
- Read initiatives
- Read progress

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Perdoo. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- OKR alignment signals
- Blocker correlation
- Goal confidence

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Perdoo** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
