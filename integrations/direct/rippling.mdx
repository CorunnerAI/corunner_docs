Rippling is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner sync employees, teams, and time off. Once connected, agents get org context and time-off awareness for correct routing and reminders.

## What Corunner does with Rippling

Agents read employee and team data to build org context, and read time-off records so they do not assign or ping people who are out of office.

## What you get

- Read employees
- Read time off

## Authentication

Connect with an API key you generate in Rippling. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Org context
- Time-off awareness

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Rippling** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
