Stripe is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner surface payments, subscriptions, and revenue. Once connected, agents send revenue signals, churn alerts, and billing context into chat.

## What Corunner does with Stripe

Agents read charges, subscriptions, and customers to answer revenue questions ("how did MRR trend this month?") and to flag churn risk. Billing context feeds customer-history lookups.

## What you get

- Read charges
- Read subscriptions
- Read customers

## Authentication

Connect with an API key (restricted key recommended) from your Stripe dashboard. Paste it into the connection card in Corunner.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Revenue signals
- Billing context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **Stripe** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
