SAP is a Direct Connection that lets Corunner read enterprise ERP data (documents and master data). Once connected, agents can reason about finance and operations without your team logging into SAP.

## What Corunner does with SAP

Agents pull ERP documents and master data. This feeds finance signals for the Finance Agent and gives operations teams a way to answer questions in chat that would normally require a trip to SAP.

## What you get

- Read documents
- Read master data

## Authentication

Connect with a **service account**. Your SAP admin creates a service account with the required read scopes and supplies its credentials in the connection card.

## What breaks if you disconnect

- Finance signals
- Operations context

## Manage this connection

Open **Integrations** in the Corunner web app, then find **SAP** under the **Direct Connections** tab.
