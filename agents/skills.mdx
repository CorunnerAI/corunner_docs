Skills are how Corunner's agents reason: reusable thinking methods that describe an approach, a domain, or a framework. Builtin skills are shared across all customers and change through pull-request review. Admins can layer **org-specific guidance** on top of any builtin skill without editing the shared version.

## Kinds of skills

The Skills page in the web app filters by kind:

- **Domain** — how to reason about a specific business area.
- **Task** — how to complete a recurring task.
- **Framework** — a named method or framework the agent should follow.
- **Provider** — how to reason about a specific integration or provider.
- **Agent** — guidance scoped to a single agent in the roster.
- **All** — the full list.

## Layer an org-specific override

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Skills">
    In the Corunner web app, go to **Skills**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter to the kind you want">
    Choose a kind (Domain, Task, Framework, Provider, Agent) or leave it on **All**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a skill">
    Click into the skill to see its current guidance. Your existing override is pre-filled if one exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your override">
    Add your organization-specific guidance and save. Corunner runs a safety scan before accepting the override. Empty or flagged content is rejected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revert an override

Reverting an override is destructive: it drops your tenant customization and returns the skill to the shared builtin behavior. Corunner asks for confirmation before reverting.

<Note>
  Authoring and reverting skill overrides is admin-only.
</Note>
