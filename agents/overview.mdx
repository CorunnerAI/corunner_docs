Corunner runs as a team of specialists, each excellent at one kind of work. You do not have to remember who does what: mention `@Corunner` anywhere and the right specialist picks up the request. The **Assistant** is the default fallback when no specialist fits.

## Core and Advanced

Agents come in two tiers:

- **Core** agents are included with any plan.
- **Advanced** agents require an advanced plan. Non-entitled Advanced agents are shown locked in the roster.

## How routing works

When you mention `@Corunner`, the system looks at the thread, your history, and the connected tools available, then routes the request to the specialist most likely to answer well. If none fits, the Assistant picks it up. You can also address a specific agent by name in your message.

## Model tiers

Each agent is tagged with a **model tier** (Nano, Small, Medium, Large). The tier is shown as a badge next to the agent's name in the roster. Tiers reflect the size and cost of the model Corunner routes that agent's work to.
