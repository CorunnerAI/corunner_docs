Standups in Corunner are run by the **Standup Agent** and configured per workspace. Once a standup is set up, the day-to-day posting and reply flow happens in chat. The web app is where admins create and edit standups.

<Note>
  Creating, editing, pausing, resuming, and deleting standups is admin-only.
</Note>

## Create a standup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agents → Standups">
    In the web app, go to **Agents** and switch to the **Standups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a channel">
    Choose the channel where the standup will run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a title">
    A short label for the standup (for example, "Platform daily").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    Choose the days of the week, local time, and timezone. Frequency shortcuts include **Daily**, **Weekdays**, **Weekly**, and **Custom**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the delivery mode">
    Delivery modes control how prompts are sent (for example, one message per user).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the deadline">
    Deadline is in minutes. Default is 120 minutes after the standup starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exclude specific members (optional)">
    Pick users who should not be prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable reminders and summary (optional)">
    Toggle **reminder** so users who have not responded get a nudge. Toggle **daily summary** and pick a time (default 17:00) to post a wrap-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sessions and risk

Each standup run creates a **session**. Sessions are automatically tagged with a **risk level**:

- **Low** — nothing unusual.
- **Medium** — some signals worth attention.
- **High** — likely blockers or slippage; check the summary.

Sessions live in the standup's history so admins can audit past runs and spot trends.

## Pause, resume, delete

Standups can be paused (no runs), resumed, or deleted from the same tab. Deleting a standup removes its schedule; historical session data remains available.
