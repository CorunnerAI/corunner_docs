Settings is where organization and workspace configuration lives. The page has six tabs: **Providers**, **Team**, **Channels**, **User Groups**, **Account**, and **Audit Log** (admin-only).

Page description in the app reads: _"Chat providers, team, channels, user groups, account, and audit."_

## The six tabs

### Providers

The chat providers configured for your organization (Slack and Microsoft Teams). Use this tab to see connected providers and to reconnect if a token expired.

### Team

Team members and their roles. See [Roles & Access](/get-started/roles-and-access) for what Owner, Admin, and Member can each do.

### Channels

The channels Corunner operates in. Adding a channel lets Corunner respond to `@Corunner` mentions there and lets standups and other automated posts run in it.

### User Groups

Restrict access to **private tools** and **private channels** by grouping the specific people who should see them. Anything shared with a group is visible only to that group's members; the rest of the workspace does not see it.

Only admins can create, rename, or delete groups and manage their membership and grants. Members can view groups and see who belongs to them, but cannot make changes.

Each group has two sections:

- **Members** — search the org directory by name or email and add teammates to the group. Removing a member cuts their access to every private tool and channel granted through this group.
- **Tool & channel access** — grant this group access to **Private tools** (made private in **Integrations**) and **Private channels** (made private in **Settings → Channels**). Only resources marked private appear in the picker, since public ones are already available to everyone.

<Note>
  Making a tool or channel private is a separate step done on the tool card in **Integrations** or on the channel row in **Settings → Channels**. User Groups is where you then decide which groups can use it.
</Note>

### Account

Organization-level account details.

### Audit Log (admin-only)

An audit trail of admin actions, integration changes, and other org-level events. Members do not see this tab; the backend also blocks the underlying data for non-admins.
