The Dashboard is the landing page in the Corunner web app. It shows the current state of your workspace at a glance: what is connected, how much of your plan you have used this month, and quick links into the sections you touch most often.

## What you see

- **Connection status**: whether your chat provider (Slack or Microsoft Teams) is connected and how many integrations are active.
- **Plan usage this month**: AI tokens, workspaces, chat providers, and standups against your plan allowance.
- **Quick links**: shortcuts into Integrations, Agents, Team & Channels, and (for owners) Billing.

## Who sees what

- **Owners and admins** see full connection status, usage bars, and admin quick links. Owners additionally see billing.
- **Members** see a lightweight version: enough to know Corunner is set up and where to go for help. Billing and admin-only shortcuts are hidden.
