The Analytics page in the Corunner web app has two tabs: **Activity** and **Billing cycle**. Activity is about what agents are doing across your workspace. Billing cycle shows how much of your plan allowance you have used this month.

## Activity

Choose a range of **7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days**. Activity covers agent runs, approval rate, and volume by agent so you can see where Corunner is doing the most work and where it is being asked to do things it cannot.

## Billing cycle

Plan-allowance usage for the current billing month:

- **AI tokens** used against your token allowance.
- **Workspaces** in use against your workspace limit.
- **Chat providers** connected against your chat-provider limit.
- **Standups** created against your standup limit.

<Note>
  Credit-consumption details live under Analytics rather than Billing so admins and members can see what agents are consuming without needing plan-management access.
</Note>
