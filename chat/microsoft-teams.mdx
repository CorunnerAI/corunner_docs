Corunner runs inside Microsoft Teams so your team can mention `@Corunner` in any channel or chat without leaving Teams. Install requires a Teams admin because the app requests tenant-wide permissions during consent.

## Install Corunner in Teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Corunner app in Teams">
    First add the Corunner app in Microsoft Teams from the Teams app store.

    <Info>
      The Corunner listing in the Teams Marketplace is coming soon. Until it is public, your Corunner contact will share the app package so a Teams admin can side-load it.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the Corunner onboarding page">
    Open the Corunner sign-in page and complete SSO with your Teams account. You will land on **Connect your workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Microsoft Teams">
    Connecting requires a Microsoft Teams admin. You will approve the permissions Corunner can access across your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not an admin? Corunner will show an **ask your admin** message and a shareable link you can send to the person who can complete the install.
</Tip>

## Mention @Corunner

Once installed, mention `@Corunner` in any channel, group chat, or one-on-one chat:

```text Teams
@Corunner summarize this thread and pull out the action items
```

Corunner streams its reasoning back into the same message thread, with citations from your connected tools.

## Approvals in the message

If a response includes an action that touches a production system, a customer, or a person, the message shows **Approve**, **Edit**, and **Reject** controls. Whoever has permission to act can respond right there. Approvals never require jumping to the web app.
