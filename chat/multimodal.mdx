Corunner reads images and documents directly inside chat. You do not have to describe what is on the screen. Post a screenshot, a diagram, a whiteboard photo, a mockup, or a document into any thread and mention `@Corunner`. Corunner identifies what is in the image, correlates it with your connected tools, and proposes the next action right in the conversation.

## What you can drop in

- Architecture and system diagrams
- Product screenshots and error dialogs
- Whiteboard photos
- Design mockups and Figma screenshots
- Documents (PDFs and images of pages)

## Example prompts

```text Architecture diagram
@Corunner what's the risk in this system?
```

```text Error screenshot
@Corunner has anyone seen this error before? Pull related incidents.
```

```text Product mockup
@Corunner file a Linear issue for the empty-state redesign, attach the mockup.
```

## What Corunner does with the image

1. Identifies the components, text, or artifacts in the image.
2. Maps them to your organizational memory: repositories, owners, past decisions, related incidents.
3. Proposes an action (file an issue, DM the owner, summarize the mockup for a review) with the image attached.
4. If the proposed action needs approval, the message shows **Approve**, **Edit**, and **Reject** controls.
