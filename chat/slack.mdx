Corunner lives inside Slack so your team can stay in the tools you already use. Once you add Corunner to your workspace, you can mention **@Corunner** in a channel, thread, or direct message to route work, ask questions, or trigger agents.

## Installing Corunner in Slack

Corunner is added to Slack during the [onboarding flow](/quickstart). The install requires a Slack workspace admin. On the onboarding screen, open the connection card and choose **Add to Slack**. Slack will ask you to approve the channels and permissions Corunner can access.

<Info>
  Members who are not workspace admins will see a shareable link they can send to an admin to complete the install.
</Info>

## How to interact with Corunner

Once installed, Corunner can be mentioned anywhere in Slack.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mention in channels" icon="slack">
    Tag **@Corunner** in a public or private channel to start reasoning about the topic. Corunner reads thread context and replies in place.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reply in threads" icon="comments">
    Keep a conversation focused by replying inside a thread. Corunner returns reasoning and actions right in the thread.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Direct messages" icon="message">
    DM Corunner for private requests, status updates, or one-on-one agent tasks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Approvals" icon="check">
    When an agent proposes an action, Approve, Edit, and Reject controls appear directly on Corunner's message.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Reasoning and approvals

When Corunner is working on something, it streams its reasoning back in the thread so you can follow the logic. If the action touches a production system, a customer, or a person, Corunner requests human approval before proceeding. You or anyone with permission can respond with Approve, Edit, or Reject right in the chat.

Learn more about [approvals in chat](/chat/approvals-in-chat) and how [governance policies](/admin/governance) control what requires approval.
