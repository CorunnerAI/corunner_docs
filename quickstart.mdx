This quickstart walks a workspace admin through installing Corunner in Slack or Microsoft Teams and completing first-run setup so your team can mention `@Corunner` in any channel or thread.

<Note>
  Installing Corunner in Slack or Teams requires a workspace admin. Members can sign in and use Corunner once the workspace is connected.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A Slack workspace or a Microsoft Teams tenant where you have admin rights.
- Access to the Corunner sign-in page for your organization.

## 1. Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Corunner sign-in page">
    Corunner uses SSO through your workspace provider. There is no password to set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Sign in with **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams**. Corunner captures which provider you signed in with, so the install flow that follows matches it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in onboarding">
    After sign-in, the app takes you to **Connect your workspace**. You will see a single install card for the provider you signed in with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Connect your workspace

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Slack">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Add to Slack">
        You need to be a Slack workspace admin. Slack will ask you to approve the channels and permissions Corunner can access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve the install">
        Complete Slack's OAuth consent screen. You return to Corunner with the workspace connected.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Teams">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add the Corunner app in Teams">
        First add the Corunner app in Microsoft Teams (from the Teams app store). Once added, come back to the Corunner onboarding page.

        <Info>
          The Teams Marketplace listing is coming soon. Your Corunner contact will share the app package if it is not yet public.
        </Info>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Connect Microsoft Teams">
        You need to be a Microsoft Teams admin. You will approve the permissions Corunner can access across your organization.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  If you are not an admin, Corunner shows you an **ask your admin** message with a shareable link you can copy and send.
</Tip>

## 3. Complete setup

After the workspace is connected, Corunner takes you to `/onboarding/setup` to finalize:

- Your **plan** and **seat policy** for the organization.
- Which chat providers, channels, and members Corunner can operate in.

Once setup is complete, the app opens the **Dashboard**.

## 4. Talk to Corunner

Go into any channel or DM in the workspace you just connected, and mention Corunner:

```text Slack or Teams
@Corunner what's blocking our launch?
```

Corunner streams its reasoning back into the thread and proposes the next action. You can **Approve**, **Edit**, or **Reject** the proposed action right there in the message.
